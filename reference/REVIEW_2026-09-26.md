### Implications for the present experiments

The strongest newly filled gap is a concrete comparison set for scale and stain
integration. [DSMIL's Fig. 2](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Li_Dual-Stream_Multiple_Instance_Learning_Network_for_Whole_Slide_Image_Classification_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf)
provides a spatial feature-fusion example. That motivates a separate comparison
against our independent-scale late fusion; it does not establish which wins
on the local cohort. HIPT adds a different question about the spatial extent
of contextual representation rather than selecting one nominal magnification.

[MADELEINE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02859) uses stains as representation-learning
views. This is a different intervention from exposing a stain label/presence mask
to a supervised event classifier. Multistain graph fusion supplies a task-time
fusion analogue. These sources broaden the design space without overriding
our observed ablation: any added branch still requires a controlled local test.

[Site-signature evidence](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-24698-1)
strengthens the rationale for acquisition controls. For SMC, compare full gold
versus Exp3-only and inspect metadata shortcuts, slide multiplicity and stain
availability before attributing high internal AUROC to rejection morphology.
The literature establishes the possibility of acquisition shortcuts; our audit
and controlled experiments must establish their local effect.

[CARE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38348670/) widens the clinical view:
predicting an operational pathology grade and identifying clinically important
injury have different reference standards. Keep the current ACR/AMR classifiers'
claims tied to their pathology labels. A future EHR-plus-image study needs a
separately specified outcome and prediction-time availability assessment.

## Project-level conclusions supported now

1. ACR labels should preserve the source 0R–3R ISHLT grade before any
   task-specific collapse.
2. Full pAMR nomenclature combines histological and immunopathological
   evidence; an H&E-only model must state that modality boundary.
3. Cardiac rejection WSI modeling is feasible, but independent-cohort
   performance varies and does not establish transportability to SMC.
4. CLAM/ABMIL, TransMIL, and UNI are defensible candidates or comparators;
   none is established as locally optimal without a controlled ablation.
5. Stain, site, scanner, magnification, and patient clustering are evaluation
   variables, not implementation details that can be assumed away.
6. GSE290577 is valuable longitudinal molecular evidence but is a selected
   tissue-core spatial-transcriptomic resource, not routine-WSI external
   validation.

## Important gaps for the next tranche

- Cardiac-specific evidence for multi-resolution or physical-MPP fusion.
- Direct comparison of pathology foundation encoders on endomyocardial biopsy
  morphology and local non-H&E stains.
- Prospective, clinical-impact, or workflow trials beyond retrospective model
  validation.
- Truly independent routine-WSI validation matching the intended SMC use case.
- Prognostic validation for future rejection using repeated biopsies and
  event-level bags.
- Calibration, decision-curve, and threshold-transportability evidence for the
  intended clinical decision.

Absence from this bootstrap index means “not reviewed yet,” not “absent from
the literature.”
